HOW THIS IS BUILT

Public evidence, edited into decisions.

01

Separate area from property

We first ask what the neighbourhood changes: beach access, noise, food density, airport friction and cross-island travel.

02

Read patterns, not one comment

Scores and repeated verified-review themes are summarized. We do not copy individual guest reviews or present one anecdote as consensus.

03

Show the trade-off

Every shortlist entry includes a reason to choose it and a reason to pause. A recommendation without a watch-out is advertising.

04

Date the evidence

This edition was reviewed on 30 August 2026. Links remain available so readers can confirm the newest score, price and timetable.
