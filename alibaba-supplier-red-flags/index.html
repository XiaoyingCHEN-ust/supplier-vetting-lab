Buyer field note · risk check

7 warning patterns · 8 minute read

Alibaba supplier red flags are questions—not verdicts.

Use each warning sign to request a specific piece of evidence. A platform badge can reduce search friction, but your order, product, bank instructions, and destination requirements still need their own checks.

01

The beneficiary changes just before payment

A new personal or third-party bank account is not automatically fraudulent, but it changes who receives your money. Pause until the supplier explains the relationship in writing and the contracting entity confirms the instruction through a channel you already trust.

Request nextBank account holder name, bank country, relationship to the contracting entity, and written confirmation from an independently verified company contact.
02

The company name shifts between documents

Compare the registered entity, quotation, invoice, email signature, business licence, and bank beneficiary character by character. Trading names and English translations can differ; unexplained legal entities should not.

Request nextA current business licence and a short entity map showing which company manufactures, exports, invoices, and receives payment.
03

Evidence stays generic

Certificates, catalogue images, and factory videos may be real but unrelated to your exact product. The question is whether the evidence supports the model, material, process, and destination market in your purchase.

Request nextA live video tied to your product brief, an identifiable sample, and complete reports whose model numbers and standards match the order.
04

The sample is excellent but the production controls are vague

A sample proves that one acceptable unit can exist. It does not prove repeatable production. Ask how the approved sample becomes the reference for materials, tolerances, inspection, and corrective action.

Request nextA signed sample approval, measurable specification sheet, inspection standard, defect classification, and pre-shipment acceptance rule.
05

Urgency replaces written terms

“Pay today to keep the price” is a commercial pressure signal. Slow down when the quote omits Incoterm, currency, tooling ownership, packaging, quality criteria, lead-time trigger, or remedies.

Request nextA revised quotation or purchase agreement that records every decision-relevant term before the deposit deadline.
06

Communication avoids verifiable channels

Messaging apps are useful for speed, but sensitive changes should be confirmed through stable company channels. Be cautious when a supplier refuses a scheduled video call, will not use a company domain, or cannot connect you with a responsible manager.

Request nextA live call from the business premises and confirmation of payment or contract changes through a previously verified contact.
07

The quote is attractive only before landed cost

A low unit price can be erased by freight, duty, testing, packaging changes, payment fees, rejects, or a small number of saleable units. Compare suppliers on the same landed-cost assumptions.

Request nextPackage dimensions, gross weight, HS-code assumption, Incoterm, testing needs, expected defect allowance, and all one-time charges.

What this check cannot tell you

No checklist can authenticate a company, predict production quality, or guarantee recovery. Use platform protections, independent inspections, legal or compliance advice, and controlled payment terms where the order warrants them.
