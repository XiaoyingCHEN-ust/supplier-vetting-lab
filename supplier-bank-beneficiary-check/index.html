Buyer field note · payment check

4-step decision path · 6 minute read

The bank beneficiary is part of the supplier decision.

A different account name may have a legitimate explanation. It also changes your payment evidence and recovery path. Treat every mismatch as an unresolved relationship until it is documented and independently confirmed.

Step 01

Write down all four identities

Do not compare only the sales contact and account name. Put the four decision-relevant identities in one view:

  • The legal entity on the business registration.
  • The contracting party on the quotation or purchase agreement.
  • The seller or exporter shown on the commercial invoice.
  • The beneficiary name and bank country on the payment instruction.
Step 02

Classify the mismatch before explaining it away

Step 03

Verify the instruction through a separate channel

If bank details arrive by email or chat, confirm them with a contact and channel established before the change. Do not use the phone number or link contained only in the change message. Save the confirmation alongside the quotation, contract, and account proof.

Useful prompt“Please explain the relationship between the contracting entity and the bank beneficiary, why this account receives our payment, and which document authorizes that arrangement.”
Step 04

Make the decision at order level

A company may exist and still be unsafe for a particular payment arrangement. Consider the amount at risk, payment stage, inspection result, contract enforceability, platform protection, bank country, and whether the beneficiary appears on your invoice or agreement.

Important boundary

This guide does not authenticate a bank account or provide legal, fraud, sanctions, tax, or financial advice. For a material order or unexplained routing, involve your bank and appropriate professionals before payment.

Evidence rule

A plausible story is not the same as a documented relationship.
  1. Record every entity.
  2. Explain every mismatch.
  3. Confirm through a known channel.
  4. Save the evidence before payment.
